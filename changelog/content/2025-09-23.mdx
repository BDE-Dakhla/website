  - El Khattat Yanja, President Dakhla-Oued Ed-Dahab Regional Council
  - Randy Scheunelann, Vice Chairman - Board of Directors - International Republican Institute

- **10:20 : Moderated Discussion - Driving Innovation through Collaborative Leadership**

This session will assess ongoing economic innovation efforts in Dakhla and underscore the importance of cross-sectoral collaboration to minimize duplication in development initiatives. It will present and evaluate innovative models and tools for multi-stakeholder coordination, with a focus on their practical application in the Dakhla context.

**Format: 45-minute moderated discussion, followed by 30 minutes Q&A**
- Yousra EL Hatib, Director Strategy - Oracle Morocco R&D Center
- Bouchaib Kiri, Regional Director of the Ministry of Industry and Trade.
- Mohamed Akmach - Director of Dakhla Moudabara Association
- Moderator : Riad Berdayi, Local Development Expert

- **11:35 : Coffee Break**
- **12:00 : Moderated Discussion - Fostering an Enabling Environment for Investment**

This session will outline current strategies to promote both local and foreign investment, while examinating the practical challenges that hinter their effectiveness. Panelists will offer insights into potential reforms to strengthen the investment climate, with space for participant input and feedback on emerging opportunities.

**Format : 45-minuted moderated discussion, followed by 30 minutes Q&A**
- Ahmed Kathir, Director of the Regional Center of Investment.
- Moulay Boutai Lambarki, Regional Advisory Body for Economic Revival and Boosting Investment
- Omar Chaabi, Executive Vice President YNNA Holding
- Moderator : Hal Ferguson, Vice President for Operations - International Republican Institute

- **13:15 : Modereated Discussion - Policy Pathways to Employment Strengthening Skills Systems for Labor Market Demand**

This session will examine how targated skills development can strenghten Dakhla's workforce and expand access to employment. The discussion will focus on governance enables such as institutional coordination, public-private partnerships, and policy innovation. Panelists will also highlight current initiatives reform.

**Format: 45-minute moderated discussion, followed by 30 minutes Q&A**
- Ahmed Salem El Guarhi, Head - Regional Delegate of the Office for Development of Cooperation
- Lhassan Ennassiri, Director of the National Agency for the Promotion of Employment and Skills (ANAPEC)
- Houda Barakate, General Director - Education for Employment EFE - Maroc
- Moderator : Lhoussine Soussi, Lecture Professor at the National School of Management and Commerce (ENCG)

- **11:35 : Final Recommendations & Closing**

This session will close the day with final recommendations resulting from the panel discussions
- Hamdi Ahl Sayed - Dakhla-Oued Eh-Dahab Regional Council
- Carrie Reasonover - MENA Deputy Director, International Republican Institute
- Moderator: Riad Berdayi, Local Development Expert
